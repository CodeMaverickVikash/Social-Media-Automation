## 1. Blueprint Catalog

### A. Men's Everyday & Smart Casual (`mens-everyday-style.png`)
* **Target Audience:** Men / Young Men (Smart Casual, Kurtas, Shirts, Ethnic Sets)
* **Composition:** `split` (solid dark panel ~44%, photo right ~56%) or `overlay` with deep midnight scrim.
* **Palette:**
  * Background/Panel: Midnight Slate `#06111A` / `#081622`
  * Typography: Clean Ivory `#FAF9F6`
  * CTA Button: Crisp hollow border (`border: 2px solid #FAF9F6`) with white text and right arrow.
  * Category Badge: `shirt` icon with vertical rule and *"Men's Wear"*.
* **Typography:**
  * Headline: Playfair Display 400, leading 1.04 (*"Everyday\nStyle.\nLasting\nConfidence."*)
  * Attribute Triplet: Montserrat 500, uppercase with wide letter spacing (*"CLASSIC | MODERN | VERSATILE"*)
* **Photo Finish:** Real male model wearing crisp tailored ethnic or smart wear.

### B. Festive Duo / Couple & Men's Ethnic (`festive-collection-duo.png`)
* **Target Audience:** Festive Celebrations (Men's Kurta Pajama, Sherwani & Women's Festive Wear)
* **Composition:** `overlay` full-bleed photo with left gradient scrim.
* **Palette:**
  * Scrim: Deep Wine / Velvet Maroon `rgba(51, 12, 13, 0.95)` fading to transparent at 68%.
  * Typography: Soft Champagne Gold `#F5CDB4` and Ivory `#FAF9F6`.
  * CTA Button: Filled Maroon `#560E24` pill with Champagne text and arrow.

* **Typography:**
