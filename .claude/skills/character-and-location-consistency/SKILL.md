# Character and Location Consistency Skill

## Purpose
Maintains a central reference library that locks character faces, hairstyles, skin textures, body proportions, clothing swatches, and architectural environments across all connected clips in a film production.

## Key Capabilities
1. **Character Consistency**:
   - Generates persistent `[CHARACTER IDENTIFIER]` anchor blocks.
   - Enforces warm olive/honey Indian skin tones with natural pores (bans airbrushed plastic textures).
   - Preserves facial geometry, hairstyle, and accessories across scene cuts.

2. **Location Consistency**:
   - Anchors physical locations: Jaipur Royal Haveli Courtyard, Amber Fort Jharokha, Luxury Boutique Atrium.
   - Enforces fixed lighting conditions (5000K golden-hour sun, diffused morning light) and directional ground contact shadows.

3. **Product Accuracy Preservation (Workflow B)**:
   - When a user uploads a garment photo, `product-preservation.ts` extracts exact hex palettes, weave textures, embroidery stitches, and cuts.
   - Strictly enforces *Product Accuracy > Creative Freedom*.
