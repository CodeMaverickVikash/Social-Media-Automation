# Monavi Collection — Voice & Captions

## Personality

**Elegant · Warm · Confident · Rooted.** Monavi talks like a trusted boutique stylist: gracious and sure of itself, never pushy or loud. Every outfit is part of the customer's story ("Wear Your Story").

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Graceful, unhurried | Shouty, ALL-CAPS, "!!!" |
| Specific about fabric, craft, fit | Vague hype ("best ever", "amazing") |
| Celebratory around festivals and weddings | Discount-first, bargain-bin |
| Inclusive of the whole family | Exclusive or snobbish |

## Language & Simple English Mandate ("Simple, Clear & Natural English")

- **Mandatory Simple English Rule:** All text on creatives (headlines, eyebrows, attribute triplets `attrs`, CTAs) AND all social captions (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp) MUST be in **simple, clear, everyday English**.
- **No Complex Jargon or Pretentious Words:** Strictly ban overly complex, archaic, or stiff vocabulary (e.g. `opulent`, `ineffable`, `ethereal`, `magnificence`, `splendorous`, `transcendental`, `quintessential`, `connoisseur`).
- **Direct & Accessible:** Use words that everyday customers and Indian families immediately understand: *"Royal Wedding Collection"*, *"Pure Silk Saree"*, *"Silk Kurta Pajama"*, *"Comfortable Fit"*, *"Tailored Cut"*, *"Shop Now"*, *"Visit Store"*.
- **Short, Scannable Sentences in Captions:** Keep sentences short (under 15 words). Warm, friendly, stylist-to-client tone. Light Hinglish touches are welcome for festive warmth (*"Shaadi season is here ✨"*, *"Perfect for your family celebration ✨"*).
- Use sentence case. Headlines can end with a full stop for poise: *"Royal Wedding Collection."*

## Caption formula

```
<Hook line — the feeling or occasion, 1 sentence>
<1–2 lines of specifics: fabric, craft, colour, fit, occasion>
<CTA — visit / DM / WhatsApp / tap link>

<3–5 hashtags on Facebook | 5–10 on Instagram>
```

Example (festive, Instagram):
> Some colours carry a celebration in them. ✨
> Our new festive anarkalis in deep maroon with hand-finished zari work, made for evenings that become memories.
> DM us or visit the store to try yours.
>
> #MonaviCollection #WearYourStory #FestiveWear #Anarkali #EthnicWear #IndianFashion

## Don'ts

- Don't invent prices, discounts, stock, store hours or addresses. Use `brand.json → contact`, or ask.
- Don't claim "handmade", "pure silk" or other fabric/craft facts unless the brief says so.
- Don't use competitor names, celebrity names or trending audio names in captions.
- Don't use more than 2 emoji in a row, and don't put emoji inside headlines on the image.
