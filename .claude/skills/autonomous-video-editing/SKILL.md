# Autonomous Video Editing Skill

## Purpose
Acts as a professional film editor and timeline manager, arranging approved AI video clips into seamless narrative sequences with purposeful transitions, balanced audio, and brand identity.

## Responsibilities
- **Timeline Sequencing**: Concatenates multiple clips into seamless narratives of any duration ($30\text{s}, 60\text{s}, 180\text{s}, 300\text{s}+$);
- **Transitions**: Applies purposeful cuts, dissolves, fade-to-black, and match-cuts.
- **Audio Mixing**: Balances background Indian instrumental music, synchronizes voiceovers, and ducks music by $-14\text{dB}$ during dialogue.
- **Brand End-Cards**: Composites vector Monavi star mark, Playfair Display headlines, and CTA buttons on dark luxury radial backgrounds.
- **Multi-Platform Outputs**:
  - Master Film: Full-length high-bitrate master ($1080\times 1920$, H.264 BT.709, CRF 16).
  - Instagram Reels/Stories: Duration capped to official API maximums ($90\text{s} / 60\text{s}$).
  - Facebook Reels/Feed: Duration aligned with feed limits ($90\text{s} / 600\text{s}$).
  - WhatsApp Status: Optimized for 60s and strictly $\le 16\text{MB}$ payload.
